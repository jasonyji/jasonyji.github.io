## About Me

Hi! I'm **Yi Ji (計懿)**, a third-year Ph.D. student in Quantitative Marketing at the <a href="https://www.hku.hk/" target="_blank">University of Hong Kong</a>, where I am fortunate to be advised by <a href="https://www.shanhhuang.com/" target="_blank">Prof. Shan Huang</a>.

Prior to joining HKU, I earned dual bachelor's degrees in Computer Science and Business Economics from the <a href="https://www.cuhk.edu.hk/english/index.html" target="_blank">Chinese University of Hong Kong</a>, where I had the privilege of working with <a href="https://www.cse.cuhk.edu.hk/~cwfu/" target="_blank">Prof. Chi-Wing Fu</a> and <a href="https://sites.google.com/view/yi-shanlee" target="_blank">Prof. Yi-Shan Lee</a>.

My research lies at the intersection of **Artificial Intelligence, Marketing, and Computational Social Science**, with a particular focus on AI Applications in Marketing and the Economics of AI. I draw on large language models (LLMs), machine learning, and field experiments to understand how AI shapes decision-making, consumer behavior, and market dynamics.

I am passionate about bridging academic research and real-world practice. Through collaborations with industry practitioners at leading technology companies, including ByteDance and Tencent, as well as emerging startups, I strive to develop research that is both academically rigorous and practically impactful.

Feel free to reach out at **yi-ji-mkt [at] connect [dot] hku [dot] hk** or **jy [at] datawake [dot] ai**. I'm always happy to connect and exchange ideas!
